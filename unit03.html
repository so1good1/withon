A recent report states an interesting fact about donations. People who donated continuously from their youth donate 12% more than those who didn't. It shows that it's very important to get used to sharing from an early age. So countries such as England include donations at their school events.

Every year, schools in England choose a place to donate. Then, they encourage their students to participate in activities to raise money. ________, students donate one penny every time they jump rope. So the more they jump rope, the more money they can donate. In addition, students sell cake and chocolate during lunchtime. They sometimes run a mini-marathon with teachers to raise money, too. Those experiences make both students and their parents proud because they believe donations can improve their society.

In England, it is schools that participate in donations the most actively. They teach students to continue sharing throughout their lives.
